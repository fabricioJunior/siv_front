## Description
SIV is a complete management system for retail stores, especially fashion and accessories. It brings together, in a single app, everything a store uses every day: selling at the counter, controlling the cash register, managing inventory, registering products and tracking results.

**Point of sale (POS)**
Ring up sales with a barcode scanner, apply discounts and accept several payment methods (Pix, card, cash, return credit and more) in the same checkout. Issue the receipt or the invoice and email it to the customer.

**Cash register**
Open and close the register, record cash drops and deposits, and reconcile the amounts, with a history of every register session and a closing receipt.

**Products, references and inventory**
Register references with colors, sizes and barcodes, set prices per price table and follow the balance of every product. Make manual stock entries and exits, run inventory counts and review the movement history. Print barcode labels.

**Orders, packing lists and consignment**
Manage sales orders and incoming goods orders, packing lists and consignment sales, with barcode-based checking.

**Electronic invoicing**
Integration to issue electronic invoices (Brazilian NF-e/NFC-e), with the status of every document and DANFE printing.

**Reports**
Revenue, ABC curve, sales by employee, active and birthday customers, purchases by customer and slow-moving products, so you can decide with data.

**Guided import**
Bring customers, references, prices, products, stock and sales from another system with a step-by-step assistant that previews what is already registered.

**For the whole store team**
Each person signs in with their own user and role-based permissions. The app runs on several terminals at once and keeps data in sync in real time.

Note: SIV requires an account (licensee) provided by Vale do Ceará. To subscribe or to try it, contact support.
